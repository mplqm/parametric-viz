# parametric-viz

**Point your phone at a flat surface. Tap. A protein appears.**

It's about the size of a marble. It's been moving inside every cell in your body your entire life. You've never been able to see it like this before.

---

## Try it

**[Open on your phone →](https://mplqm.github.io/parametric-viz)**

No app. No sign-up. Just open the link and tap the surface in front of you.

Works on iPhone and Android.

---

## What am I looking at?

A protein called adenylate kinase. It's a molecular machine — one of thousands running inside every living cell right now, including yours.

It has two positions: open and closed. It snaps between them millions of times per second, moving energy around so your cells can do work.

The colours show the structure from one end (violet) to the other (red). The motion you're watching is real — derived from 942 actual laboratory measurements, processed into 313 distinct shapes, with 30,735 animated transitions between them.

---

## Why does this exist?

Most scientific data lives in databases that require specialist software, domain knowledge, and institutional access to use.

This project takes that data and turns it into something anyone can hold in their hands. Built in collaboration with Claude — as architect, coder, and thinking partner throughout.

Not a research tool. A window.

The goal is the same thing Google Earth did for geography — make the shape of things feel real, at scale, in space.
